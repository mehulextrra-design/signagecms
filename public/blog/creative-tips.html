Screen Creative Design Guide — 7 Tips Jo Actually Kaam Karte Hain

90% advertisers ek simple galti karte hain — apna Instagram post 1:1 crop karke screen par laga dete hain. Screen creative alag hota hai. Ye 7 rules follow karo, result 3x better hoga.

Pehle: Screen Specs Samajhna Zaroori Hai

ExtrraVenue network ke most screens portrait orientation mein hain — yani vertical. Standard resolution 1080×1920 pixels (9:16 aspect ratio). Kuch screens landscape (1920×1080) bhi hain — venue dashboard par confirmation lena.

7 Design Rules Jo Actually Convert Karte Hain

1

3 Seconds Rule — Ek Glance Mein Message Clear Ho

Venue visitor screen ke paas 3–5 second ruk sakta hai. Agar aapka message 3 second mein samajh nahi aaya, toh ad fail hai. Rule: ek headline (max 6 words), ek visual, ek action. Kuch aur mat daalo.

2

Font Size — Jo Duur Se Dikhe

Screen se viewing distance 2–4 meter hoti hai. Minimum font size 80px (at 1080px canvas width) rakho. 60px se chhota text blur lagta hai. Condensed/thin weight avoid karo — bold ya regular hi use karo.

3

High Contrast — Backlighting Ka Account Karo

Venue screens backlit hoti hain aur sometimes direct sunlight ya bright ambient light ka saamna karna padta hai. White text on dark background best contrast deta hai. Light gray on white — kabhi nahi. Contrast ratio 4.5:1 se kam mat rakho.

4

Safe Zone — Edges Chhodo

Screen ke edges par mounting brackets ya bezels ho sakte hain. Important content — headline, offer, QR — screen ke center 80% area mein rakho. 10% all-around safe margin maintain karo.

5

QR Code — Bottom Third Mein, Minimum 25% Canvas

QR scan ke liye phone camera screen se 30–60 cm door hona padta hai. QR size 25–30% canvas width se kam mat rakho. Placement: bottom area best hai kyunki logo natural neeche dekhta hai scan karte waqt. White background on QR mandatory hai — dark background par QR often fail karta hai.

6

Animation — Subtle aur Purposeful Rakho

Flash ya rapid animation irritating aur off-putting hota hai venue environment mein. Smooth fade-in (0.5s), gentle pan ya zoom (3–5s total) better hai. Video mein 30s se zyada mat rakho — aadhe log itna nahi dekhte. First 3 seconds mein main message aa jaana chahiye.

7

Brand Consistency — Colors aur Logo Properly Use Karo

Logo top-left ya top-center, minimum 15% canvas width. Brand colors use karo lekin ensure karo wo screen par readable hain — kuch pastel colors screen par washed out lagte hain. Preview feature use karo before submit.
