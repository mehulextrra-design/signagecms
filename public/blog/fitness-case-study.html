Ahmedabad Supplement Brand Ne 3x Reach Paaya — Gym Screen Campaign Ka Full Breakdown

₹12,000 ka budget, 3 gym screens, 30 din, aur result: 1.8 lakh impressions aur 340 verified new customers — QR tracking se. Yahan unka exact strategy hai.

Background: Brand Kaun Tha, Problem Kya Thi

Ahmedabad mein ek protein supplement retail brand hai jo mainly online sell karta tha — website aur Instagram par. Footfall badhana chahte the apne Satellite area ke store mein. Traditional options — pamphlets, Instagram ads, local newspaper — tried karke dekh chuke the. Conversions aate the lekin cost per acquisition zyada tha.

Unka core audience: 20–35 saal ke fitness-conscious log. Problem tha ki ye audience social media par ad ko scroll-past kar dete the. Kuch aisa chahiye tha jo unhe tab mile jab wo already fitness mindset mein hain — yaani gym mein.

Strategy: Screen Selection Kyun Critical Tha

extrravenue platform par usable screens browse karte waqt brand ne specifically Satellite aur Bodakdev area ke gyms select kiye — their store se 2–3 km radius mein. Reasoning simple tha: gym member jo screen dekhe, wo already fitness ke baare mein soch raha hai. Supplement ad dekhega toh mindset match karta hai.

Screens Selected

  • FitZone Satellite — 280 active members, 6am–10pm hours
  • IronHouse Gym Bodakdev — 190 members, premium equipment area screen
  • PowerFit Vastrapur — 320 members, reception + floor screens

Teen screens, alag-alag locations, same target demographic. Combined daily footfall estimate: 400–500 unique visitors.

Creative: Jo Kaam Kiya

Brand ne do creatives test kiye. Pehla — product photo aur nutrition facts. Doosra — before/after transformation + offer + QR code for first-time discount.

Doosra creative significantly better perform kiya. Learnings:

  • Transformation visual gym environment mein instantly relatable lagta hai
  • Specific offer (15% off + free shaker) scanning ka reason deta hai
  • QR code size kam se kam 25% screen area hona chahiye taki gym mein se scan ho sake
  • Minimum text — 3 lines se zyada nahi, bold font, high contrast

"QR se hame exact pata chala ki kitne log store mein aye — pehli baar humne measurable ROI dekha kisi physical channel par."

Results: 30-Day Campaign Data

extrravenue dashboard par Proof of Play data:

  • Total plays recorded: 5,400 (3 screens × 30 days × ~60 daily plays)
  • Estimated impressions: 1,81,000 (screen dwell time × average visitors per play)
  • QR code scans tracked: 412
  • Conversions (store visit + purchase): 340 verified via unique discount code
  • Average order value: ₹1,850
  • Total revenue from campaign: ₹6,29,000
  • ROAS (Return on Ad Spend): 52x

Kya Zyada Kaam Kiya

Campaign ke baad brand ne kuch interesting patterns note kiye. Tuesday aur Thursday ko QR scans highest the — probably kyunki ye mid-week motivation peak days hote hain gym mein. Morning 6–8am aur evening 6–9pm slots mein conversion rate 40% better tha compared to afternoon.

Ye insight unhone next campaign mein use kiya — time-specific ad scheduling se budget 20% optimize kar paye same impressions par.

Aap Ye Kaise Replicate Karein

  1. Venue match karein: Apne product/service ka natural fit kaunsi venue type se hai?
  2. Tight geography: Apne store/office se 2–3km radius ke screens select karein — local intent zyada hoti hai
  3. QR har creative mein: Ye aapka measurement tool hai, isliye must-have hai
  4. Offer specific rakho: "Visit our store" koi nahi karega. "Scan karo, 15% off pao aaj" kaam karta hai
  5. 30-day minimum: Frequency matters — ek hi screen member ko 15–20 baar dekhne ke baad hi action leta hai
