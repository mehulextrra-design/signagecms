Salon Owner Ne Apni Screen Se ?11,000/Month Extra Kamaye — Bina Kuch Kiye

Jaipur ke Pink City area ki ek salon owner ne sirf apni waiting area ki screen extrravenue network se connect ki — aur pehle mahine se hi passive income shuru ho gayi. Unki real numbers yahan hain.

Shuruat Kaise Hua

Priya Sharma ki salon Jaipur ke C-scheme area mein hai — ek busy commercial locality. Unke waiting area mein ek 43-inch TV tha jo sirf news channel chalata tha. Ek extrravenue team member ne unhe venue partner program ke baare mein bataya.

Priya ka pehla reaction tha: "Mujhe kuch karna hoga toh nahi? Clients ka experience toh kharab nahi hoga?"

Answer tha — bilkul nahi. extrravenue screen par ek Android player device laga deta hai, usko internet se connect karta hai, aur aage ka kaam platform automatically karta hai. Priya ko bas apna WiFi password dena tha.

"Pehle mahine mein ?9,400 aaye. Dusre mahine ?11,200. Mujhe literally kuch nahi karna pada — sirf apna bank details dene the."

Revenue Kaise Calculate Hota Hai

extrravenue ka venue partner revenue model simple hai: jab bhi koi advertiser aapki screen par ad play karta hai, aapko uska ek fix percentage milta hai. Ye percentage aapki screen ki zone aur traffic rating par depend karta hai:

  • Zone A screens (high-traffic: malls, gyms, busy cafes): 35% ad earnings
  • Zone B screens (medium-traffic: salons, clinics, restaurants): 30% ad earnings
  • Zone C screens (emerging: smaller venues): 25% ad earnings

Priya ki salon Zone B mein thi — 30% share. Unke screen ka daily rate ?800/day tha (platform ne set kiya based on location aur footfall estimate).

Venue Own Content — Extra Income Ka Source

extrravenue venue partners ke paas ek additional revenue stream bhi hota hai: venue_own content. Priya ne apne salon ke promotions — new service launches, Diwali offers, referral program — khud upload kiye. Ye content platform ke "filler" slots mein play hota hai.

Is se unhe do fayde mile:

  • Salon ke apne promotions customers tak pahunche waiting area mein hi
  • Filler content play hone par bhi minimum revenue credit hota hai

Venue Partner Banne Ki Requirements

  • Indoor venue with waiting/common area (min. 200 sqft)
  • Consistent footfall — min. 30 visitors/day
  • Reliable WiFi (minimum 10 Mbps)
  • Power outlet near screen location
  • Willingness to keep screen on during operating hours

Clients Ka Experience: Kaisa Laga

Priya ka concern tha ki clients irritate nahi honge. 6 months baad unka feedback:

"Actually ulta hua. Clients ab screen dekhte hain waiting mein — kuch interesting ads aate hain toh wo mujhse baat karte hain. Ek baar ek client ne mujhe bola ki unhone screen par ek new cafe ka ad dekha aur baad mein gaye bhi. Atmosphere better laga unhe."
