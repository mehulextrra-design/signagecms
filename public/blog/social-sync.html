Ek Upload, Teen Jagah — Instagram, Facebook aur Screen Par Ek Saath

ExtrraVenue ka Social Sync feature aapka content workflow simplify karta hai. Ek creative upload karo — platform automatically Instagram story, Facebook post aur venue screens par simultaneously publish karta hai.

Problem: Har Platform Ke Liye Alag Kaam

Ek typical small business owner ka content workflow kuch aisa hota tha: designer se Instagram story banwao (9:16 format), phir Facebook post ke liye alag crop (1:1 ya 4:5), phir screen ke liye alag version (1080×1920 with different text sizing). Teen alag uploads, teen alag scheduling tools, teen alag logins.

ExtrraVenue ka Social Sync isko ek step mein karta hai.

"Pehle teen platforms ke liye ek-ek kaam karna padta tha — ab ek baar upload karo aur sab ho jata hai. Time 70% save hoti hai."

Social Sync Kaise Kaam Karta Hai

1

Creative Upload Karo (9:16 format)

ExtrraVenue dashboard par apna vertical creative upload karo — ye screen format ke saath Instagram story format bhi hai, toh ek hi file kaam karti hai.

2

Platforms Select Karo

Checkboxes: Instagram Story / Facebook Post / Venue Screens. Sab select karo ya sirf jo chahiye.

3

Schedule Set Karo

Ek date/time — sab platforms par simultaneously publish hoga. Ya alag-alag time bhi set kar sakte ho per platform.

4

Platform Auto-Formats

Facebook post ke liye platform automatically caption add karta hai. Screen version ke liye duration set karta hai. Sab background mein hota hai.

Platform-Wise Kya Hota Hai

📸

Instagram Story

9:16 creative directly published. 24 hours visible. Link sticker optional.

📘

Facebook Post

Auto-cropped for feed (4:5). Caption from campaign name. Scheduled post, not story.

📺

Venue Screen

Original 9:16 file. Duration 10s (image) or 30s (video). Proof of Play tracked.

Kaun Ise Sabse Zyada Use Karta Hai

Social Sync sabse zyada useful hai un brands ke liye jo regularly promotions run karte hain — restaurants (weekly specials), fashion brands (new arrivals), fitness centers (class schedules), clinics (seasonal health camps).

Ek campaign banana ka time 20 minutes se ghatke 5 minutes reh jata hai. Design, upload, schedule — done.
