Digital Signage Kya Hai? Nayi Zamane Ka Marketing — Complete Guide

Digital screens par apna ad dikhana sirf bade brands ke liye nahi raha. Aaj gyms, salons, cafes aur clinics mein bhi screens hain jahan aap exactly apne target customer tak pahunch sakte ho — real-time mein, transparent pricing par.

Digital Signage Ka Simple Matlab

Digital signage matlab hai electronic screens par dynamic content display karna — jaise hoardings ke bajaaye TV screens ya LED panels. Ye screens internet se connected hoti hain, toh aap inka content remotely change kar sakte ho bina physically wahaan gaye.

Traditional hoarding mein ek baar print ho jaata tha toh 30 din waisa hi rahta tha. Digital screen mein aap kal ka offer aaj raat update kar sakte ho, Monday ko alag ad dikha sakte ho aur weekend ko alag — sab remotely, sab instantly.

"Digital signage traditional print se 47% zyada effective hai brand recall ke liye — isliye globally DOOH (Digital Out-of-Home) advertising sabse fast-growing marketing channel ban gayi hai."

Ye Kahan Kaam Karta Hai?

Digital signage sirf malls aur airports par nahi hoti. extrravenue ke network mein ye venue types hain:

  • Gyms aur Fitness Centers — members workout karte waqt screen dekhte hain, average dwell time 45+ minutes
  • Salons aur Beauty Parlors — waiting time mein customers screens par dhyan dete hain
  • Cafes aur Restaurants — khana khate ya wait karte waqt ad exposure
  • Clinics aur Hospitals — waiting room mein high attention, relevant audience
  • Co-working Spaces — professionals ka daily traffic

Traditional Hoarding Se Kaise Better Hai?

Digital Signage vs Traditional Print — Fark

  • Targeting: Print = sab ko dikho. Digital = apne exact audience ke venue pe dikho
  • Cost: Print mein design + printing + installation. Digital mein sirf creative upload
  • Flexibility: Print = 30 din fixed. Digital = kab bhi change karo, kab bhi band karo
  • Proof: Print mein koi guarantee nahi. Digital mein har play ka exact timestamp
  • Minimum spend: Print mein lakhs lagti thi. Digital mein ₹1,999 se shuru
  • Speed: Print mein 1-2 week. Digital mein aaj approve karo, kal se live

Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? — Technical Side Simply

extrravenue par advertise karne ka process teen steps mein hota hai:

  1. Creative Upload karein: Apna image (JPG/PNG) ya video (MP4) upload karein. 1080×1920 portrait format recommended hai.
  2. Screens aur Dates Select karein: Kaun se venues, kaun si city, kitne din — sab aap decide karte ho. Real-time pricing dikhti hai.
  3. Payment aur Live: Online payment karo — UPI, card, sab accept hota hai. Approval ke baad campaign live ho jaati hai aur screens par play hona shuru.

Piche server par kya hota hai: extrravenue ka system har screen par connected Android TV player ke through content push karta hai. Player every few minutes schedule check karta hai aur updated content automatically play karna shuru kar deta hai.

Proof of Play — Kaise Pata Chalega Ki Ad Actually Chali?

Ye woh cheez hai jo extrravenue ko traditional advertising se fundamentally alag banati hai. Har baar jab aapka ad kisi screen par play hota hai, system us play ka exact timestamp, screen name, duration, aur play count record karta hai. Ye data aapke dashboard mein real-time mein available hai.

Matlab agar aapne 1,000 plays book kiye hain toh aap verify kar sakte ho ki exactly 1,000 plays hue — minute-by-minute breakdown ke saath. CSV mein export karo, WhatsApp par share karo, client ko dikhao.

Kitna Cost Aata Hai?

extrravenue par pricing fully transparent hai — koi hidden fees nahi. Cost depend karti hai venue type, location aur campaign duration par. Generally:

  • Trial package: ₹1,999 (7 din, 1 screen)
  • Regular 30-day campaign: ₹6,000–₹15,000 per screen (location ke hisaab se)
  • Multi-screen campaigns: volume discount milta hai

Effective CPM (cost per 1000 impressions) typically ₹5–₹12 range mein hota hai — Google Display ya Facebook ads se comparable, lekin physical venue ka trust factor zyada.
