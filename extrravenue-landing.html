Screen advertising
actually aisa kaam karta hai

Jo cheezein sirf extrravenue par milti hain — aur kyun ye dusre options se alag hai.

01

Mall mein log screen ko ignore nahi kar sakte

Social media par ad aaya — skip kar diya. Mall mein khade ho — screen ke saamne ho, kuch nahi kar sakte. Yahi fark hai. Captive audience hoti hai — woh genuinely dekh rahi hoti hai, scroll nahi kar rahi.

02

Screen se seedha aapke WhatsApp mein inquiry

Har ad ke saath QR code hota hai. Koi interested ho, scan kare — seedha aapke WhatsApp par aa jaata hai. Baith ke wait nahi karna padta. Aur dashboard mein dikhta hai kitne logo ne scan kiya — engagement ka real data.

03

Ek payment — Screen, Facebook aur Instagram teeno

Yahan sirf screen book nahi hoti. Jab ad live hota hai, automatically Facebook page aur Instagram par bhi post hota hai. Teen platforms ek saath — alag alag karna padta to teen guna kaam aur paisa lagta.

04

Ek screen, ek din se shuru karo — koi lock-in nahi

Bade commitment ki zaroorat nahi. Pehle ek screen, 1-2 din ke liye try karo. Results dekho. Phir apni marzi se badhao ya wahi raho. Koi contract, koi minimum spend, koi penalty nahi.

05

Sirf premium spots — wahan jahan log actually jaate hain

Hamare screens mall, corporate offices aur busy markets mein hain — yahan daily hazaron ki footfall hoti hai. 100 choti jagah se ek busy mall ki screen zyada kaam aati hai. Reach quality mein hoti hai, sirf quantity mein nahi.

06

Ad chala — proof haath mein, koi doubt nahi

Date, time, kitni baar chala — sab record hota hai. "Proof of Play" report download karo — black and white data. Client ko dikhana ho, ya khud rakhna ho — ready hai. Sirf baat nahi, actual data.

Abhi shuru karo →

Portal mein jaao, screen chunao — wahan dikhta hai har screen ki rate. Jitni screens, jitne din — total GST ke saath sab calculate hokar screen par dikhta hai. Pay karne se pehle koi surprise nahi.

Photo — JPG ya PNG. Video — MP4. Size 200 MB tak chal sakta hai. Upload karo aur hum check karte hain ki screen par sahi dikhega ya nahi — phir live hota hai.

Wallet mein paise dalo — phir jab bhi campaign book karo tab wallet se pay ho jaata hai. Baar baar card nahi daalna padta. Agar balance bacha ho aur waapis chahiye, to UPI ya bank account mein request kar sakte ho.

Pay karte hi email par aa jaata hai — invoice number, amount, GST sab ke saath. Accountant ko dene ke liye ready. Baad mein bhi billing page se download kar sakte ho.

Dashboard mein "Reports" mein jaao — wahan dikhega kab chala, kitni baar chala, kaunsi screen par. CSV mein download karo aur client ko bhej do ya khud rakh lo.

Bilkul. Venue partner ke roop mein register karo. Aapki screen platform par list ho jaayegi. Advertisers uspe ad chalayenge aur aapko har play ka share milega — khud kuch nahi karna.
